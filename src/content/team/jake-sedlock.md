An accredited auctioneer trained at Nashville Auction Academy, Jake performs
benefit auctions across the country. He began auctioneering at Elena's
elementary school, where he discovered his passion for being on stage and
raising funds. With his improv training, stand-up comedy experience, and
infectious enthusiasm, Jake motivates donors and ensures everyone has a great
time.
